Read the curve, find the sensor

A hidden sensor was chosen at random and its response was measured, with a little noise. Work backwards from the curve, the way an engineer does with a real sensor, and estimate its parameters. Then check your guess.

First order:   y(t) = K A (1 − e−t/τ)     Second order:   G(s) = K ωn² / (s² + 2ζωn s + ωn²)
Tools you have: hover over any point to read (t, y), and click two points to measure Δt and Δy. For a first-order step, the log-method helper plots ln(1 − y/K) against t, which is a straight line with slope −1/τ.
