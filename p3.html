How performance falls as Ts grows

The same gains and plant are run again at many sampling times. The marker shows the Ts you chose. While Ts is small the digital loop behaves like the continuous one. As Ts grows the held command reacts late, the overshoot rises, and past some Ts the loop no longer settles.
