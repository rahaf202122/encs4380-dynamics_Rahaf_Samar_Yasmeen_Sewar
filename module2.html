3. Understanding the Results

Amplitude Ratio:

M = 1 / √(1 + (ωτ)²)

Phase Angle:

φ = -tan⁻¹(ωτ)

Dynamic Error:

δ = M - 1

The output signal is:

y(t) = K A M sin(ωt + φ)
Remember: A negative phase angle means that the output signal lags behind the input signal. A smaller amplitude ratio means that the system reduces the input amplitude more.
